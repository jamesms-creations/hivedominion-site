Privacy Policy

Hive Dominion (iOS app com.jamesmscreations.hivedominion)
Last updated: 2026-10-05

This policy explains what data the Hive Dominion game collects, why, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it. Hive Dominion is operated by {{OPERATOR_NAME}} ("we", "us"). If you have any question, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

The short version

  • You don't create an account with a name, email or password. The game creates an anonymous account for your device the first time it runs.
  • We store your account's random ID and your game progress on our server so the game can work. That's it.
  • No ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and we never sell or share your data for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and all its game data at any time in the app: Settings → Delete Account.

1. What we collect and why

DataWhat it isWhy we need it
Account identifiers A random account ID and profile ID created for your game account. They are not linked to your name, email, phone number, Apple ID or Game Center profile. To know which game progress is yours.
Sign-in credentials The app creates a random install credential and receives session tokens from our server. Our server stores only one-way hashes of these, never the values themselves. They are not your device's hardware or advertising identifiers (we do not collect the IDFA or IDFV). To sign your device in to your account securely.
Gameplay content Your hive layout, army, resources, progress, battle and Supply Lines results, and battle replays. To save your progress, run the game's server-checked battles and rewards, and prevent cheating.
Time-zone offset Your device's offset from UTC (for example "+60 minutes"), not your location. To time daily events and show clocks correctly.
Purchase history (when in-app purchases are available) For each purchase: Apple's transaction ID, the product bought, the date, the in-game items delivered, and whether it was later refunded. Apple handles payment; we never receive your card details, billing address or Apple ID. To deliver what you bought exactly once, handle refunds, and keep records required for tax and accounting.
Sign in with Apple (when available, and only if you choose to use it) The anonymous user identifier Apple provides for this app, and an encrypted token Apple issues so the link can be revoked. We do not request your name or email address. To let you recover your account on a new or reset device.
Crash reports When the app crashes or hits an error, Unity Cloud Diagnostics (a service of Unity Technologies, the maker of the game engine) receives a technical report: the error and stack trace, app version, device model and OS version. These reports are not linked to your game account. To find and fix bugs.
Server request logs For each request to our server: the action requested, the result, how long it took, and the account ID. We do not write IP addresses or sign-in tokens to these logs. To keep the service running, investigate errors and stop abuse.
Emails you send us Your email address and whatever you write. To answer you.

IP addresses. Like any internet service, our server and our network provider (Cloudflare) see your IP address when the app connects. Our server uses it only in memory, for example to limit how many new accounts can be created from one address per hour. We do not store IP addresses in our database or request logs.

What we don't collect: your name, email address (unless you email us), phone number, contacts, photos, precise or approximate location, advertising identifier, device identifiers, browsing history, or health or financial information. The game has no chat and no user-generated content.

No tracking, no ads, no selling. Hive Dominion contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites (so it never shows Apple's tracking permission prompt). We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are used in US state privacy laws, and we do not use your data for profiling or automated decisions about you.

2. Legal bases (EU, UK and similar laws)

  • To provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract): account identifiers, sign-in credentials, gameplay content, time-zone offset, purchase history and Sign in with Apple.
  • Our legitimate interests in keeping the game secure, working and fair: request logs, transient IP use for abuse prevention, and crash reports.
  • Legal obligations: keeping purchase records for tax and accounting.

Our servers are located in the United States. Cloudflare and Unity operate globally and may process data in the United States and other countries. If you play from outside the United States, including from the EU, UK or Switzerland, your data is transferred to the United States. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) where a provider is certified under it, or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Settings → Delete Account and confirm with Delete. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, its sign-in identities and sessions, and all its game data from our live database. The app then forgets its sign-in credentials and local save and starts fresh as a new player. Deletion cannot be undone. Remaining copies age out of backups and logs as described above, and purchase records are kept only in de-identified form.

By email: you can also ask us to delete your data at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Because accounts are anonymous, we may need you to help us identify the right account, and we may not be able to match an email to an account at all; deleting from inside the app always works.

Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device (the install credential, session token and local save) but does not delete your account on our server. Use Delete Account first if you want the server data gone too.

8. Children

Hive Dominion is rated 9+ and is not directed at children under 13. The game does not ask for anyone's age, name, email, phone number, location, photos or other contact details, has no chat or way to share content with other players, shows no ads, and does no tracking. The only data it handles is the anonymous account and gameplay data described above, used only to run the game, support it and keep it secure.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age set by the law of your country, which in the EU can be up to 16) beyond what is strictly needed to operate the game. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, for example in a support email, contact us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will delete it. You can also delete your child's game account at any time in the app (Settings → Delete Account).

9. Security

All connections between the app and our server are encrypted (HTTPS). We store only hashes of sign-in credentials, keep backups encrypted at rest, and limit administrative access to our servers. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data, and if a breach occurs we will notify the authorities and, where we can reach them, affected players, as the law requires.
