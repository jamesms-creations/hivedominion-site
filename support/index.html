Support

Need help with Hive Dominion? Check the questions below, or email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

Your account

Do I need to sign up?

No. The first time you open the game, it creates an anonymous account for your device automatically. There is no username, email or password. Hive Dominion is an online game, so it needs an internet connection to play.

I reinstalled the game or got a new phone. Can I get my progress back?

Not yet, unfortunately. Your account is tied to the game's install on your device. If you uninstall the game, reset your phone or move to a new phone, the game starts a new account, and because accounts are anonymous we have no way to verify which old account was yours, so we can't move it for you.

Sign in with Apple is coming in a future update. Once you link it to your account, you'll be able to sign back in to the same hive after a reinstall or on a new device. Until then, please don't uninstall the game if you want to keep your progress.

How do I delete my account?

In the game, open Settings → Delete Account and confirm with Delete. Your account and all its game data are deleted from our servers immediately, and the game restarts as a new player. This can't be undone. Remaining backup copies expire within 21 days; see the Privacy Policy for details.

You can also email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} to ask for deletion, but because accounts are anonymous we may not be able to find yours from an email. Deleting in the app always works.

Uninstalling the game does not delete your account on our server.

Purchases

What can I buy?

Hive Dominion is free to play. Optional in-app purchases (such as Royal Jelly packs and a one-time Starter Pack) are being added. Purchases are handled by Apple and charged to your Apple Account; we never see your payment details.

I bought something but didn't receive it.

Purchases are delivered to your game account by our server after Apple confirms them. If an item hasn't arrived, make sure you're online and restart the game: unfinished purchases are retried automatically when the game starts. If it still hasn't arrived after that, email us with the date, the item and your Apple receipt (the order ID from Apple's email), and we'll sort it out.

Can I restore my purchases?

Items you buy (such as Royal Jelly) are added to your game account and stored on our server, so they stay with that account. They are consumed in the game and are not "restored" like an app download. If a future item can be restored, a Restore Purchases button will be shown in the in-game shop.

Because accounts are currently tied to your device (see above), purchases made on an account are lost with it if the game is uninstalled before Sign in with Apple is available. Deleting your account also removes everything bought on it.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple refunds a purchase, the items it granted are removed from your game account.

Other questions

The game says it can't connect.

Hive Dominion needs an internet connection. Check your Wi-Fi or mobile data and try again. If the server is down for maintenance, the game will say so; please try again a little later.

Does the game have ads, chat or tracking?

No. There are no ads, no chat, no user-generated content and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
